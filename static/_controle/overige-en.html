Welcome to DeNode Foundation

The DeNode Foundation is a home for contemporary art in the heart of Ghent. Through exhibitions, residencies and international collaborations we give a place to artists, to research and to the encounters around it. To us, art is not a luxury but a way of looking at the world more attentively.

Address, opening hours and contact: see Visit.

Sign up for our newsletter or social media and stay updated on future events and exhibitions!

Subscribe via the newsletter form or follow us on Instagram.
