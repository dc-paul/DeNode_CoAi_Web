Dear art lover,

Now / Highlight

Humanimality

Monsieur Térez

16 Oct – 20 Dec 2026 · opening Fri 16/10, 6 – 9 pm

This Friday, 16 October, Monsieur Térez’s solo exhibition Humanimality opens. For a month DeNode Foundation was his Fabulisterie; now the Humanimals populate the gallery: eccentric, fable-like creatures between human and animal. Opening from 6 to 9 pm — everyone welcome, no registration needed. The exhibition runs until 20 December.

Open call · deadline Wed 18/11, 11.59 pm

Humain, trop humain

Act I

Are you an artist, or do you know one? Until Wednesday 18 November (11.59 pm) the open call is running for Humain, trop humain — Act I, the first exhibition in DeNode Foundation’s new cycle, under the artistic direction of Kristof Vander Cruyssen. All information at www.denode.be/opencall — and feel free to forward this mail.

Open call →
 

Album release · Fri 27/11

Vestiges

Robrecht Kessels

On Friday 27 November cellist Robrecht Kessels presents his vinyl record Vestiges at Academy One Studios, Antwerpsesteenweg 197, 9040 Ghent. Doors 7.30 pm, live at 8 pm. Free, but registration is required (button below). The LP is on pre-sale (€ 29) via the Vestiges page in the shop.

7.30 pm doors · 8.00 pm live set
9.00 pm reception · 10.00 pm DJ Spanky

G.AN.D.A. — that same evening the G.AN.D.A. art route opens; the gallery stays open until 10 pm that day.

Register →
 

Agenda

Fri 16 Oct

  • 6 – 9 pm — Opening Humanimality, Monsieur Térez · Predikherenlei 4

16 Oct – 20 Dec

  • Exhibition Humanimality

Sat 7, 14, 21 and 28 Nov

  • De Fabulisterie, drop-in workshops on Saturdays — on four Saturdays in November (7, 14, 21 and 28 November), each time from 2 to 4 pm, you can come to the exhibition Humanimality, Predikherenlei 4, to paint, write and model. Free, no registration, materials provided: just walk in. Guided by Kristof Vander Cruyssen. On 28 November the workshop coincides with the first G.AN.D.A. weekend: combine it with the other exhibitions on the art route.

Wed 18 Nov

  • 11.59 pm — Last day of the open call Humain, trop humain, Act I · for artists

  • Doors 7.30 pm — Album release Vestiges, Robrecht Kessels · Academy One Studios, Antwerpsesteenweg 197, Ghent · free, registration required (150 places)
