allesgut

Can a book become an exhibition?

With allesgut, Björn Wandels presents a project in which book and exhibition are two forms of one autonomous artwork. The artist book grew out of a years-long process of collecting, ordering and reordering images. For Wandels, images never stand alone; they always point to other images, to memories and to possible new connections.

The exhibition translates the logic of the book into space. Images, sequences and connections are released from the book and take on a new spatial constellation in the exhibition room. After the vitrine projects of 2024, allesgut is Björn Wandels' first solo exhibition at DeNode Foundation.

The artist book is published by MER Books in co-production with DeNode Foundation, in a limited edition of 400 copies. The scenography of the exhibition was designed by Anja Hellebaut.

€ 38 if you collect it · € 45 shipped (€ 38 + € 7 postage). The book is on sale from now on: receive it at the opening, collect it afterwards at the foundation, or we send it to you.

Five free workshops: Saturday 19 and 26 September, Saturday 3 October, Wednesday 7 October and Saturday 10 October, each from 2 to 5.30 pm. For everyone, young and old, and you can sign up via the form on the website.
