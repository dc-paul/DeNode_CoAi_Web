OPENING OF NODENAYSTEEN — AN ART SPACE

An art space in Ghent
Paul De Cannière

WE ARE JUST STARTING

This new place in the artistic landscape of Ghent invites diverse artists with their work from a love for art, music, technology, and much more. Located on the Leie, with a view of the Sint-Michielskerk and 't Pand (UGent), Nodenaysteen is a space dedicated to discovery, learning, and nurturing relationships with artists, collectors, enthusiasts, and passersby.

The name Nodenaysteen refers to the old stone, of which a remnant can still be seen in the (little) garden. Traces of this patrician house can be found in the city archives dating back to 1357.

Nodenaysteen aims to embrace the future through engaging collaborations in the realm of art and technology while maintaining a connection with the history.
