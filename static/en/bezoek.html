Visit

nodenaysteen / DeNode sits on the river Leie, in the heart of Ghent — by St Michael's Bridge, a few minutes from the Korenmarkt. The street is car-free: the last stretch is always on foot, along the water.

Address

Galerie DeNode — nodenaysteen

Predikherenlei 4, 9000 Ghent

On foot

From the Korenmarkt, walk towards St Michael's Bridge and take the quay down to your left just before the bridge: that is Predikherenlei — number 4 sits on the waterfront, directly opposite St Michael's Church and Het Pand (± 3 minutes, without crossing the water). From Ghent-Sint-Pieters railway station it is a ± 30-minute walk through the city centre.

By bike

By bike you get almost to the door. Park free of charge and under cover in the Korenmarkt bicycle parking — literally underneath St Michael's Bridge, one minute from the gallery (210 spaces, max. 48 hours).

By tram or bus

The Gent Korenmarkt stop is ± 250 metres away. Please note: due to major works in Ghent's city centre (2026–2029) tram and bus services change regularly — plan your journey in advance via delijn.be. From Ghent-Sint-Pieters station there is always a connection towards Korenmarkt.

By car

You cannot drive up to the door: the gallery lies in the car-free zone and in Ghent's low-emission zone (LEZ). Park at Parking Sint-Michiels (Sint-Michielsplein 8) — underground, open 24/7, 465 spaces, a ± 3-minute walk from the gallery: cross St Michael's Bridge and turn right into Predikherenlei on the other side. Follow "zone Korenmarkt" on the digital road signs.

Good to know: the car park uses number-plate recognition (no ticket), payment is electronic only, maximum entry height is 2.00 m and LPG vehicles are not allowed. Check the live occupancy before you leave.

Accessibility

nodenaysteen is a historic building: not all spaces are wheelchair-accessible yet — we are working on it. Parking Sint-Michiels has 3 accessible parking spaces and a lift. Let us know in advance via info@denode.be or +32 488 88 08 89 and we will make sure you can see as much as possible.

Sunday: 11 am - 5 pm
